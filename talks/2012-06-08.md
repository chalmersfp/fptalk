This walk will be in two parts.  The first won't have anything to do
with definitional interpreters but will highlight some of my past work
that may connect to the interests of functional programmers at Chalmers.
The second part will describe some work in progress with Jan Midtgaard
and Matthew Russell.  It will address these questions:

- How can we connect formal semantics with simple interpreters?

- What kinds of representation choices and program transformations
might yield significant performance improvements for minimal
effort?

- What combination of representation and code performs best?

- ***Why?***

The third and especially fourth questions are unanswered, and I will
ask for guidance about what sorts of answers might be most
interesting.

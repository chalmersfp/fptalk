Proofs found by most automated theorem provers are hard to read, for two reasons. Firstly, the proof is by contradiction, which makes it hard to see how the goal is reached. Secondly, the proof steps make heavy use of unification, which is hard for a person to follow. We would like to take these complicated machine-generated proofs and transform them into a simple, human-friendly style that does not use proof by contradiction or unification. I will present our recent progress on this problem for the Horn fragment of first-order logic. This is joint work with Lydia Kondylidou, Jasmin Blanchette and Uwe Waldmann.

Sequential decision problems are a well established concept in decision theory, with the Bellman equation as a popular choice for describing them. Botta, Jansson, Ionescu have formalised the notion of such problems in Idris. Here we focus on an Algebra of SDPs (in Agda): combinators for building more complex SDPs from simpler ones. Joint work with Robert Krook, U. of Gothenburg. Extended abstract presented at TyDe'19.

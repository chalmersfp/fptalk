title: "Reduceron Presto"
place: "EE"
date: 2011-02-25 10:15:00
---
The Reduceron is a graph-reduction machine for functional-program computation.  A redesign for single clock-cycle reduction of function application combined with dynamic analysis for speculation made the 2010 design 5x faster than the 2007 original.  But two of the main design objectives remain in conflict.  This talk will explain the 2010 Reduceron and this conflict, then outline Reduceron Presto, a further redesign of speculation with promising initial results.

> In programming, there are often pairs of functions running in opposite directions: the domain of one is the
> codomain of the other. Their functionalities are so closely related that it is usually possible to derive one from
> the implementation of the other. Bidirectionalization techniques address this concern. This thesis studies some
> of the theoretical and practical aspects of bidirectionalization.
> As the theoretical part of this thesis, we generalize an existing bidirectionalization technique, known as
> semantic bidirectionalization. Our generalized algorithm scales well and lifts some of the restrictions set by the
> original algorithm.
> As the practical part of this thesis, we focus on the problem of tracking expressions in the
> low-level generated code to their origins in the high-level code.

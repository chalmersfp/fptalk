title: "Type error diagnosis in Helium, the Haskell compiler for novice Haskell programmers"
place: "EDIT"
date: 2013-03-01 10:00:00
---
This talk consists of two parts: in one I discuss how we attained Domain
Specific Error Diagnosis in Haskell 98. In the other I discuss further
heuristics that are employed in the Helium compiler for improving type error
diagnosis. This work dates back to 2003, but recently I have brushed off these
ideas and hope to obtain funding to  extend it to work for Haskell with its
many extensions. Moreover, we are also in the process of making Helium ready
for Hackage.

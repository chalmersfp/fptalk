title: "Haskell's Data Parallel Options when Implementing Error  Correcting Codes"
place: "8103"
date: 2013-12-17 11:00:00
---
Haskell has many options for parallel execution, including data parallelism.  In this talk, we will look at a Haskell implementation of the Low Density Parity Check (LDPC) error-correcting code, and explore ways of speeding in up using existing Haskell libraries for parallelism. We will report on our initial findings and challenges, and how equational reasoning helps us reach more efficient solutions.

This is joint work with KU PhD student Mike Stees.

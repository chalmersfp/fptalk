I present as part of my Master's thesis a proof of concept back-end for the GHC compiler. The back-end is based on the Jive compiler, which utilizes a new data-flow based intermediate representation called the Value State Dependence Graph (VSDG). The VSDG is a directed graph consisting of nodes representing operations, conditionals and loops, and two kind of edges representing value- and state-dependencies between the nodes. This representation is superior to traditional control-flow based intermediate representations, since it is signicantly cheaper on which to perform optimizations. Traditionally, GHC desugars Haskell to the Core language, a functional intermediate representation close to System F. This is then further transformed to the representation of the STG machine and
to C- - before nally the old back-ends take over. However, the new back-end does not follow this traditional path, and rather starts o directly with the Core language. This makes it possible to abandon several idiosyncrasies and restrictions introduced by the STG machine.

speaker: "Ivar Lång"
title: "A Parallel Intermediate Representation for Embedded Languages"
place: "8103"
date: 2013-05-30 10:00:00
---
This work presents an intermediate representation for parallelism called PIRE,
and its incorporation into the Feldspar language. The current Feldspar backend
compiles to C, but parallel loops are flattened to regular for-loops. With the
new PIRE backend we instead explore the use of OpenCL as a target language.
Parallel loops are compiled to OpenCL kernels that can be off-loaded to GPUs
(or other OpenCL-capable devices). We evaluate the performance of PIRE in an
experimental setting by comparing it to the original Feldspar backend as well
as hand-crafted GPU code.

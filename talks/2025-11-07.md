Although today’s Large Language Models (LLMs) are powerful, they often generate factually incorrect or outdated information and struggle to explain what they truly “know.” Knowledge Graphs (KGs), on the other hand, store verified facts and relationships in a transparent and easily updatable structure.
This talk explores how combining KGs with LLMs can address these weaknesses, enabling more accurate and explainable reasoning. Focusing on Knowledge Graph Question Answering (KGQA), we examine how to retrieve relevant knowledge from KGs and integrate it into LLM prompts. The study systematically analyzes how different LLM architectures, KG retrieval methods, and prompt engineering strategies influence the quality of answers. The results highlight a promising direction for building reliable and knowledge-grounded AI systems.

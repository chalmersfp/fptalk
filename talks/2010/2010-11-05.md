speaker: "Michał Pałka"
title: "Random generation of simply-typed lambda terms"
place: "EE"
date: 2010-11-05 10:15:00
---
Compilers and language tools are complex and difficult to test pieces of
software. It is in particular difficult to generate programs that could serve
as test data. In this talk I will present a generator of random simply-typed
lambda terms that is aimed at solving this problem. The mechanism of generation
as well as unsolved issues will be discussed in addition to preliminary
results from testing the GHC compiler.

This talk will present work on a formal semantics for the programming language Timber. I will start with a short overview of Timber and then introduce a stratified design where pure expressions are separated from imperative and concurrent commands, and demonstrate how the two parts interact to form a single language. Finally I will discuss how this comparatively full-featured language can be extended with constructs and semantics for time-correct execution.

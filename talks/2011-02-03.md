place: "EDIT room"
date: 2011-02-03 14:15:00
---
Haskell is a wonderful language well know for its static assurance of application correctness. We decided to use Haskell for our application: signing documents in an online service that we called skrivapa.se. We went the everything-Haskell route: web server, document storage, document processing, external services connection. In this talk we would like to share our experiences, upsides and downsides of using Haskell for a middle scale commercial project.

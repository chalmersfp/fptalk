The new edition of the Swedish spelling dictionary SAOL (Svenska Akademiens ordlista) will be published early next year. I have been helping out by writing automatic tests to find mistakes in the dictionary. Although it is hard at first to see how to specify
a dictionary, there are a surprising number of things that can be formally checked. I will talk about what I have tested, the bugs I have found, the troubles with testing unstructured data, and (perhaps) about what a library for property-based testing of the
dictionary could look like.

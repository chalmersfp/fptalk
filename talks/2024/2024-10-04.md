This talk presents the notion of causal-consistent reversible debugging
and its instance on Erlang provided by CauDEr. Reversible debugging allows
us to explore an execution back and forth looking for the bug causing a
visible misbehavior. Causal-consistent debugging tailors this approach to
concurrent systems, where backward execution is not forced to undo the
actions of a forward execution in reverse order, but can undo any action
provided that its consequences, if any, are undone beforehand. CauDEr is a
causal-consistent reversible debugger for the functional and concurrent
language Erlang.

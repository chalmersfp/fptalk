StarCraft 2 is a real-time strategy game that is played as a professional e-sport. It also has an active bot scene, with a 24/7 ladder and regular tournaments [1]. We'll talk about the game, and why writing a bot for it is a fun and interesting problem, and show how we solved some of the many (many) interesting subproblems in our Haskell bot Xena.

[1] https://aiarena.net/

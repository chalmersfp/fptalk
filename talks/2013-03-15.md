speaker: "Björn von Sydow"
title: "Real numbers and intervals in Haskell"
place: "EDIT room"
date: 2013-03-15 10:00:00
---
I will present a Haskell module for arbitrary precision real arithmetic, which I am developing. I will discuss some design decisions and the general ideas of the implementation, and also give some motivating examples.  Part of this motivation comes from validated numerics, which uses interval arithmetic and automatic differentiation in novel ways to compute rigorous solutions to many numeric problems.

The talk will also describe a dark corner of the present implementation, where I would be happy to be enlightened by new ideas.

This presentation-tutorial is a practical session to introduce 
[Strudel](https://strudel.cc/) - a live coding platform and language originally built with Haskell, and ported to JavaScript. Strudel is a flexible language for coding music (and visuals) in the browser, and is open-source. In this presentation-tutorial, we will introduce the
language and engage hands-on with coding music and visuals. This session will be heavily practical, so please bring a laptop and headphones to fully engage with the interactive components of the session.

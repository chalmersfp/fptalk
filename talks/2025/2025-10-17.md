Last year I got an idea for a simple chat-based computer game involving Large Language Models (LLMs): if someone sent you a text message, could you tell if it was coming from a person or an LLM? Over the winter I implemented the game as a website with Haskell for the backend and PureScript for the frontend. In this talk I want to present the game to you, how I made it, and have you play a game or two!

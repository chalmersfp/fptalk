This presentation explores the capabilities and limitations of Large Language Models (LLMs) as foundation AI systems. We examine how these models generate human-like text by recognizing patterns in vast training data, yet fundamentally operate as sophisticated
text prediction engines rather than true reasoning systems. Through practical examples, we demonstrate their limitations in logical reasoning and fact reliability. We introduce our Chain of Relational Algebra (CoRA) approach, successfully implemented at Volvo
as GateLens for automotive software validation, which reduced analysis time by 80% while maintaining high reliability for safety-critical decisions. The presentation concludes by examining emerging approaches like vector-based reasoning that may define the
next generation of foundation models, offering more robust solutions for complex reasoning tasks.

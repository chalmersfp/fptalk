We present our FPGA-based processor, Heron, for evaluating non-strict functional
programs, as well as its concurrent garbage collector. Among the growing group
of contemporary projects, Heron offers an attempt at implementing a template
instantiation machine to compete with general purpose platforms (not
necessarily embedded). We'll touch on its architecture, reflect on our design
choices, and our plans towards a multi-core prototype.
